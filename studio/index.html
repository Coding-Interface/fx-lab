PhaserFX Studio

1 · Pick or start

Choose an example on the left, or press New. Examples are read-only; your changes save as a new preset in My presets.

2 · Tune it live

Every change in the inspector replays on the stage. Click the stage to move or fire the effect. Use slow motion to study it. Undo / Redo (Ctrl/⌘+Z, Ctrl/⌘+Shift+Z) step back through your edits.

3 · Use it in your game

Copy the code from the Use in game panel. Load PhaserFX, then one line plays your preset: this.fx.particles(preset, x, y).
